In my opinion, school uniforms do more harm than good and should not be compulsory. Although they are meant to create equality, they often achieve the opposite.

First, uniforms are expensive. For instance, a full uniform can cost over one hundred dollars, which is a serious burden for low income families. Because of this, some students wear worn or ill-fitting uniforms and feel ashamed.

Second, uniforms remove a way for young people to express who they are. Dress is an important part of identity, and forcing everyone to look the same can feel restrictive. Opponents argue that without uniforms students will compete over brands; however, a well designed dress code prevents this without removing choice.

In summary, because uniforms are costly and reduce freedom, schools should allow students to wear their own clothes.
