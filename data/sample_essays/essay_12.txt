School uniforms have good points and bad points. On one hand they make everyone look the same, wich can reduce bullying. On the other hand they are expensive and students do not like them very much.

I think the main problem is cost. Some families cannot afford a proper uniform, and this can make students feel left out. However, schools could help by giving money to families who need it, or by making uniforms cheaper.

In the end, I think uniforms can stay if they are cheap, but if they cost too much then schools should let students wear normal clothes.
