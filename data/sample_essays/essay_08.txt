School uniforms should not be abolished. Many people think they are expensive but in the long run they can actually save money because you do not need to buy lots of different clothes. Uniforms also make everyone look the same so there is less bullying about what people wear. Statisics show that schools with uniforms have less bullying and better behaviour. Also it is easier in the morning because you do not have to decide what to wear and you are not late. I strongly believe uniforms are a good idea and they should stay in all schools.
