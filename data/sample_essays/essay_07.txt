I think remote learning is bad. When you are at home it is very easy to get distracted. There is nobody to make you work, so you can just stop whenever you want. Also you cannot ask the teacher questions as easily as in a classroom.

Another problem is that some students do not have good internet or a quiet room. This means they cannot join lessons propely and they fall behind the other students. This is definately not fair to them.

Some people say that remote learning is good becuase you can sleep more and you do not have to travel. But I think the bad things are more important. In the end I think most students learn better at normal school.
