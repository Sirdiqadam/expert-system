School uniforms should be abolished because they are expensive and students dislike them. Uniforms cost families a lot of money each year, and this is hard for parents who do not earn very much. Buying new uniforms every time a child grows is a real problem.

Another reason is that uniforms stop students from expressing themselves. Many teenagers want to choose their own clothes and show their personality. In addition, uniforms do not seem to improve behaviour as some people claim.

However, some argue that uniforms make everyone equal. This is a fair point, but I still think freedom is more important than sameness.

In conclusion, I believe schools should let students wear their own clothes because it is cheaper and fairer for everyone.
