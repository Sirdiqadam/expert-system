Uniforms should be banned in schools. They are too expensive for many families and students do not like wearing exactly the same clothes as everyone else. Uniforms can also be uncomfortable in hot weather, which is a problem in the summer term.

Some people think uniforms are good because they make the school look smart and tidy. However, I think students should be allowed to decide what to wear, within reason. A simple dress code would keep things sensible without forcing everyone to buy an expensive uniform.

In conclusion, I think uniforms should not be required and schools should trust students to dress appropriately.
