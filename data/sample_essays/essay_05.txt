Remote learning is not good for students. It is hard to concentrate at home because there are many distractions like television, games and family noise. Students also miss their friends and feel lonely, wich makes learning even harder.

Some people say remote learning is flexible and saves travel time. That is true, but I think the problems are bigger. For example, not everyone has a good computer or reliable internet, so some students fall behind and find it difficult to catch up again.

To conclude, remote learning has some advantages but I think normal school is better for most students.
